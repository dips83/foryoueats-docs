  <Accordion title="How much does Foryoueats cost?">
    Foryoueats is completely free — no hidden fees. You get unlimited recipes, unlimited shopping lists, and a full-featured meal planner, all at no cost.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Are my recipes private?">
    That totally depends on you. Your recipes start out **Private**: only you and the Foodie Friends you add can see them. If you are feeling generous, switch to **Public** to share them with everyone. New cooks are asked when they join, and you can change it anytime in **Settings → Share**. You can also share a single private recipe with anyone by sending them its link.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why choose Foryoueats as your recipe manager?">
    Foryoueats gives you unlimited recipes, unlimited shopping lists, and a full meal planner — all for free. Our ratings are amongst the best in our category, and we are proud of the community we are building. But beyond the app, we have a bigger dream: to one day use community contributions to help donate and feed the hungry. Behind it all, it's just my wife, our son and me, building this from our kitchen table with a shared love of good food — for everyone.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
