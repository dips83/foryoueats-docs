If you've forgotten your password, you can set a new one in a couple of minutes.

<Warning>
  You can't reset your password while you're signed in to Foryoueats in the app or in a web browser on your device. If you are, the reset page and link take you straight to your account instead of letting you choose a new password. Sign out first from **Menu → Logout**.
</Warning>

## Reset your password

<Steps>
  <Step title="Request a reset link">
    Go to [foryoueats.com/forgot-password](https://foryoueats.com/forgot-password), enter the email address you signed up with, and tap **Email Password Reset Link**. You can also get there from **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the email">
    Look for an email from **support@foryoueats.com** and click the reset link. The link works for **60 minutes**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a new password">
    Enter your new password, then sign in to the app with it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I didn't get the email">
    - Check your **spam** or **junk** folder.
    - Make sure you entered the same email address you signed up with. If no account uses that email, the page tells you straight away and no email is sent.
    - Wait a minute before asking for another link. You can only request one reset email per minute.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The link says it's invalid or expired">
    Reset links expire after 60 minutes, and only the newest link works. Request a fresh link at [foryoueats.com/forgot-password](https://foryoueats.com/forgot-password) and use the most recent email.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I signed up with Google, Facebook, or Apple">
    If you signed up with Google, Facebook or Apple, you don't have a Foryoueats password. Sign in by tapping the same button you used to sign up: **Login using Google**, **Login using Facebook** or **Login using Apple**.

    **Using the iPhone app?** It only has Apple and email sign-in. If you signed up with Google or Facebook:

    1. Tap **Forgot your password?** and enter the email address of your Google or Facebook account.
    2. Open the email we send you and choose a new password.
    3. Back in the app, sign in with that email address and your new password.

    Your recipes will all be there. If you sign in and your account looks empty, you've probably signed in to a different account. See [Can't find your recipes?](/account/lost-recipes)
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
