## Everything you need, in one place

Foryoueats gives you a complete toolkit for managing your cooking life — free to use and ready when you are. You can browse community recipes without an account; saving, importing and planning need a free account.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Unlimited Recipes" icon="book-open">
    Store as many recipes as you want, forever. Import from websites, create from photos, or type them in manually.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Meal Planner" icon="calendar-days">
    Plan breakfast, lunch and dinner on a monthly calendar, using your saved recipes.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Shopping Lists" icon="cart-shopping">
    Generate shopping lists from your recipes. Share them with Pantry Pals for collaborative grocery runs.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Pantry Manager" icon="box-archive">
    Keep track of what's in your pantry, and share it with your Pantry Pals.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Foodie Friends" icon="share-nodes">
    Add Foodie Friends to let them see your private recipes. You can also let them edit your recipes or share your meal plans.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Get started in three steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Sign up at [foryoueats.com](https://foryoueats.com/register) using your email, Google, Facebook, or Apple account (in the iPhone app, Apple or email). If you sign up with email, confirm it from the link we send you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your first recipe">
    Paste a URL from any recipe website to import it instantly, snap a photo of a cookbook page, or create a recipe from scratch using the recipe editor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start cooking">
    Check your recipe, create a shopping list, get your ingredients and get to cooking.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Already using another app?

Switching from another recipe manager like Plan To Eat, Paprika, or Copy Me That? You can bring your recipes with you using our bulk import tool. We support a growing list of apps — and if yours isn't on the list yet, let us know and we'll do our best to add it.
