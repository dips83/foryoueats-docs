---
title: "Edit and Delete Recipes in Foryoueats"
description: "Update an existing recipe's details or remove it from your library. Edit ingredients, steps, tags, and timing, or delete recipes you no longer need — one at a time or several at once."
---

Recipes in your library aren't set in stone. You can reopen your recipes to fix a typo, adjust ingredients, or add tags — and remove recipes you no longer want to keep. Both options live in the same place: the **⋮** options menu at the top right of the recipe photo.

<Note>
  You can edit your own recipes, and a Foodie Friend's if they've let you edit their recipes. Only you can delete your recipes.
</Note>

## Edit a recipe

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the recipe">
    Go to the recipe page for the recipe you want to edit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the options menu">
    Tap the **⋮** at the top right of the recipe photo to open the recipe options.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Edit">
    Select **Edit** to open the recipe in the editor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make your changes">
    Update any field — name, description, photo, ingredients, steps, timing, servings, tags, notes, linked recipes, privacy, or nutrition facts. Each field works the same way as when you first created the recipe.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your changes">
    Tap **Save Changes** to update the recipe in your library, or **Cancel** to leave it as it was. (On a draft, you'll see **Save Draft** and **Create** instead.)
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Delete a recipe

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the recipe">
    Go to the recipe page for the recipe you want to delete.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the options menu">
    Tap the **⋮** at the top right of the recipe photo to open the recipe options.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Delete">
    Select **Delete** from the options menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    You'll be asked **Delete Recipe?** Tap **Delete** to remove it. You'll be taken back to My Recipes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Delete several recipes at once

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select recipes">
    In **My Recipes**, press and hold a recipe on your phone, or hover over a recipe on a computer and tap the **✓**. Then tap any other recipes you want to include.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Actions, then Delete">
    Tap **Actions** in the bar at the bottom, then **Delete**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    You'll be asked **Delete Recipes?** Tap **Delete**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Only your own recipes are deleted. Any Foodie Friends' recipes in your selection are left alone.

<Warning>
  Deleting a recipe is permanent — it can't be undone. If you might want the recipe later, consider keeping it instead.
</Warning>
