When you want to enter a recipe yourself (from memory, a family card, or your own creation), the recipe editor gives you a full set of fields to build a complete recipe.

## Create a recipe from scratch

Tap the green **+** button at the bottom right of your recipe pages, then select **Start From Scratch** to open the recipe editor. On a computer you can also click **Create Recipe** under Recipes in the left sidebar.

## Fill in the recipe details

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add a photo (optional)">
    Tap **Add Image** to upload a photo of your dish.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name your recipe">
    Enter a clear, descriptive name in the **Name** field. This is what appears in your library and search results.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a description (optional)">
    Write a short description to remind yourself, or tell others, what makes this recipe worth cooking.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set servings and times">
    Fill in **Servings**, then **Prep Time**, **Cook Time** and **Total Time** in hours and minutes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter ingredients">
    Add each ingredient on its own line. Include the quantity, unit, and ingredient name (for example: *2 cups all-purpose flour*). End a line with a colon (:) to make it a section heading.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the steps">
    Enter each step in order, and tap **Add Step** for the next one. Drag ⋮⋮ to reorder steps. End a short step with a colon (:) to make it a section heading.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add more (optional)">
    Under **More**, add **Tags**, **Notes**, a **URL**, or **Link Recipes** to connect related recipes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your recipe">
    Tap **Create** to add the recipe to your library, or **Save Draft** to finish it later.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Recipe settings

At the bottom of the editor, under **Settings**:

- **Keep it private** hides this recipe, even if your recipes are public, and from your Foodie Friends too.
- **Set nutrition facts myself** lets you enter nutrition per serving. Leave it off and Foryoueats works out the nutrition facts with AI.

## Privacy settings

Control who can see your recipes from **Settings → Share**. On a computer, click your avatar in the top right; on a phone, tap **Menu** in the bottom bar.

| Setting | Who can see your recipes |
|---|---|
| **Private** | Only you and the Foodie Friends you add |
| **Public** | Everyone on Foryoueats |

Your recipes are private unless you switch to public.
