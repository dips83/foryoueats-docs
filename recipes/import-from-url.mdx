---
title: "Import a Recipe from a Link"
description: "Paste a link from a food blog, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and more, and Foryoueats fills in the recipe for you to check and save as your own copy."
---

Foryoueats can import recipes from food blogs and recipe sites, as well as Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, YouTube and Pinterest. Paste the link, and Foryoueats reads the recipe and fills it in for you. Once you save it, it's your own copy, so you never lose a recipe if the original site goes down or changes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy the recipe link">
    Open the recipe in your browser or app and copy its link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Import From a Link">
    In Foryoueats, tap the green **+** button (or **Create Recipe**) and select **Import From a Link**. On a phone you can also tap **Import** in the bottom bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste and import">
    Paste the link into the field and tap **Import**. If the field is empty, the button says **Paste & Import** and pastes the link for you. Foryoueats reads the recipe and opens it in the Create Recipe form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and save">
    Check the recipe, make any edits you want, then tap **Create**. To finish it later, tap **Save Draft** instead.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  You own your saved copy. Even if the original website removes the recipe or goes offline, your version stays in your account.
</Note>

## What gets imported

- The recipe's name, ingredients, steps, servings and times
- The recipe photo and tags, where the site has them
- Nutrition facts, which are worked out with AI after you save (unless you turn on **Set nutrition facts myself** and enter them)

For Instagram, TikTok, Facebook and YouTube, the recipe needs to be written in the caption or spoken in the video. A Pinterest pin is followed through to the website it came from.

<Info>
  Recipes you import from a link are saved for you (and your Foodie Friends) and never appear in the public community.
</Info>

## Import while browsing with the Chrome extension

The Foryoueats Chrome extension lets you save recipes with one click without leaving the page you're on.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install the extension">
    Install the Foryoueats extension from the Chrome Web Store.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Browse to a recipe">
    Navigate to any recipe page in Chrome.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the extension icon">
    Click the Foryoueats icon in your browser toolbar, then click **Import Recipe**. You'll be taken to Foryoueats where you can check the recipe and tap **Create**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  Firefox and Safari extensions are coming soon. Until then, use **Import From a Link** on those browsers.
</Info>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The import failed or the recipe came in incomplete">
    Some websites block us from reading them or structure their pages in unusual ways. Try copying the recipe text and using the **Start From Scratch** option instead, or take a screenshot and use **Create From Image**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The ingredients or steps look garbled">
    Use the recipe editor to clean up any formatting issues after import. Open the recipe page, tap the ⋮ button in the top right of the photo, and select **Edit**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
