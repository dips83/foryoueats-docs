---
title: "Tag and Filter Your Recipes"
description: "Add tags to your recipes to organize and filter your library — create your own or reuse existing ones."
---

Tags let you label recipes with anything that's meaningful to you — cuisine type, dietary preference, occasion, cooking method, or whatever helps you find what you're looking for. You can add tags when creating a recipe or any time you edit one.

## Add tags to a recipe

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the recipe editor">
    Create a new recipe, or open an existing one, tap the **⋮** on its photo and tap **Edit**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the Tags field">
    Scroll to the **Tags** field, under **More** in the editor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a tag">
    Tap **Find a tag** and start typing. Tap a matching tag to add it, or tap **Add** to make a new tag from what you typed. Suggested tags, including your favorite tags, also appear under the field — tap one to add it instantly.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To take a tag off, tap the **×** on it. With more than one tag, **Clear All** removes them all.

<Note>
  You can add up to 15 tags per recipe, and each tag can be up to 4 words.
</Note>

## Filter your library by tag

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Filter">
    In **My Recipes**, tap **Filter** (next to **Sort**).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Filter by Tag">
    Tap **Filter by Tag** to see your tags. You can search the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap a tag">
    Tap a tag and your library shows only recipes with that tag.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The active tag appears as a chip next to **Sort** and **Filter** — tap its **×** to clear the filter. You can also choose **Any Tag** under **Filter by Tag**.

## Favorite tags

Your favorite tags appear as one-tap chips beside **Sort** and **Filter** on **My Recipes**, and as suggestions when you're editing a recipe — so the tags you use most are always one tap away.

To set your favorite tags, go to **Account Settings → Favorite Tags** and add the ones you reach for most often.
