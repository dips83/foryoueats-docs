---
title: "Cook Mode"
description: "A focused, distraction-free view for cooking — keeps your screen on and shows a recipe's ingredients and steps together."
---

Cook Mode gives you a clean, focused view of a recipe while you cook. Where your browser supports it, your screen stays on automatically so you never have to tap it awake mid-recipe.

## Start Cook Mode

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a recipe">
    Navigate to any recipe you have access to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Cook Mode">
    Open the **Steps** tab on your phone (or scroll to **Steps** on a computer) and tap the **Cook Mode** button at the top right of the steps.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start cooking">
    You'll be taken to the Cook Mode view, where ingredients and steps are shown together. Your screen will stay on while you cook.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Cook Mode is available to signed-in users with a verified account.
</Note>

## What you see in Cook Mode

On a computer, ingredients and steps are stacked one after the other on the same page. On a phone, they're split into two tabs — **Ingredients** and **Steps** — and you can tap or swipe between them.

If you prefer the stacked layout on your phone, turn off **Account Settings → Recipes → Recipe tabs**.

The recipe name and photo appear at the top so you always know which recipe you're on. As you go, tap a step to mark it done, and you can still use **Scale** and **Convert** on the ingredients.

## Cook multiple recipes at once

You can add more than one recipe to Cook Mode — useful when a meal has multiple components.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the first recipe">
    Tap **Cook Mode** on the first recipe. You'll land on the Cook Mode page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add more recipes">
    Go back and open another recipe, then tap **Cook Mode** again. It's added to your Cook Mode session without removing the first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch between recipes">
    The recipe at the top of the Cook Mode page shows **Tap to switch**. Tap it and choose any other recipe from **Switch Recipe**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Finish cooking

When you're done with a recipe, tap **Done Cooking** at the bottom of the page. This removes it from your Cook Mode session.

- If you had multiple recipes, you'll stay in Cook Mode on one of your other recipes.
- If it was your last recipe, you'll be taken back to the full recipe page.

Tap **Full Recipe** at any time to see the complete recipe page. This doesn't end Cook Mode — tap the chef-hat icon to get back to it, and use **Done Cooking** when you've finished.
