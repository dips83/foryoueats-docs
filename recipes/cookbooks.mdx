---
title: "Create Cookbooks and Organize Your Recipes"
description: "Group your saved recipes into cookbooks — for meal planning, special occasions, or any collection you want to keep together."
---

Cookbooks let you organize recipes from your library into named collections. Create as many as you need and add any saved recipe to one or more of them.

## Create a cookbook

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Recipes, then Cookbooks">
    In the app, navigate to **Recipes** and select **Cookbooks**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap the green plus button">
    Tap the large green **+** button in the bottom right corner. (If you don't have any cookbooks yet, you can also tap **New Cookbook**.)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name your cookbook">
    Enter a name, add a cover photo with **Add Image** if you like, and tap **Create**. Your cookbook is ready to fill.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Add a recipe to a cookbook

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the recipe in your library">
    Browse your library or search for the recipe. It must already be saved to your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the recipe options">
    Tap the **⋮** at the top right of the recipe's photo — on its card, or on your recipe's own page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Add To Cookbook">
    Tap **Add To Cookbook** and choose the cookbook you want to add it to.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Only recipes saved to your library can be added to a cookbook. If you found a recipe through community search, save it first and then add it to a cookbook. **Add To Cookbook** appears once you have at least one cookbook.
</Note>

## Add several recipes at once

In **My Recipes**, press and hold a recipe on your phone, or hover over a recipe on a computer and tap the **✓**. Tap any other recipes you want, then tap **Actions** → **Add To Cookbook** and choose the cookbook.

## Remove a recipe from a cookbook

Removing a recipe from a cookbook does not delete the recipe from your library — it only removes it from that collection.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the cookbook">
    Tap **Cookbooks** and select the cookbook that contains the recipe.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the recipe options">
    Tap the **⋮** on the recipe you want to remove.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Remove it">
    Tap **Remove From Cookbook**, then tap **Remove** to confirm.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Rename or delete a cookbook

Tap the **⋮** on a cookbook (on its card in **Cookbooks**, or at the top of the cookbook's page):

- **Edit** lets you change its name or cover photo.
- **Delete** removes the cookbook after you confirm. The recipes in it aren't deleted.
