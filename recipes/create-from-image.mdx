---
title: "Create a Recipe from a Photo or Image"
description: "Upload up to 5 photos of a recipe (a cookbook page, handwritten card, screenshot or even a finished dish) and Foryoueats turns them into a recipe using AI."
---

You can create recipes from physical sources without typing them out by hand. Foryoueats uses AI to read your images and fill in the recipe for you: name, servings, times, ingredients and steps.

## What you can import

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Cookbook pages" icon="book-open">
    Photograph a page from any cookbook or magazine. A recipe across two pages? Add both.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Handwritten cards" icon="pencil">
    Snap a photo of a recipe card, even if the handwriting isn't perfect.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Screenshots" icon="image">
    Upload a screenshot of a recipe from any app or website.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Dish photos" icon="utensils">
    Upload a photo of a finished dish and the AI works out the dish and writes ingredients and steps for it.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

You can add up to 5 images of one recipe. Each image can be up to 10MB, in JPG, PNG or WebP format. Several images are combined into a single recipe.

## How to import

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Create From Image">
    In Foryoueats, tap the green **+** button (or **Create Recipe**) and select **Create From Image**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your images">
    Drop your images into the upload box, or tap it to browse your device. Then tap **Continue**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for processing">
    Foryoueats reads the images with AI and opens the recipe in the Create Recipe form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the result">
    Check each field (name, servings, times, ingredients and steps) to make sure everything was captured correctly. If servings or times weren't written down, the AI estimates them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit and save">
    Make any corrections, then tap **Create** to add the recipe to your library, or **Save Draft** to finish it later.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Your first image becomes the recipe's photo. If that's a cookbook page or recipe card, you may want to swap it for a photo of the food.
</Note>

<Tip>
  For best results, photograph the recipe in good lighting and make sure the text is in focus. Avoid shadows across the page.
</Tip>

<Warning>
  AI extraction is accurate but not perfect. Always review the ingredients and steps before cooking from an imported recipe, especially quantities and measurements.
</Warning>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Sorry, we could not generate a recipe from this image.">
    The AI couldn't find a recipe in your images. Try a clearer, closer photo of the recipe text, or use **Start From Scratch** to type it in.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
