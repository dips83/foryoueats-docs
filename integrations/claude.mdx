---
title: "Connect Claude"
description: "Connect Claude, the AI assistant, to Foryoueats to find and save recipes and fill your shopping lists, pantry and meal plan from a chat."
---

Connect Claude, the AI assistant made by Anthropic, to your Foryoueats account. Then ask Claude in a chat and it works with your Foryoueats recipes and adds things straight to Foryoueats:

- **Find your recipes:** ask what you have, get ideas from your own recipes, or have one scaled or changed.
- **Fix up a recipe:** have Claude write the ingredients or steps a recipe is missing, tidy them up, or scale it. You see the changes in the chat and tap **Save**.
- **Save a recipe** you made or worked out together to your library.
- **Add to a shopping list:** items, or all the ingredients of a recipe in your library.
- **Stock your pantry** from a receipt, a photo or your shopping list.
- **Plan meals** on your meal planner.

Claude can't delete anything in your account, and it only changes a recipe when you review the change and save it.

Gemini and Muse are coming soon.

## Connect Claude

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Connected Apps">
    In Foryoueats, go to **Account Settings → Connected Apps** and tap **Connect Claude**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Foryoueats in Claude">
    Tap **Open Claude**. Claude opens with Foryoueats already filled in. Check the address is `foryoueats.com/mcp`, then tap **Continue** and **Add**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Allow it in Foryoueats">
    Sign in to Foryoueats if asked, then tap **Connect**. When it says **Claude is connected**, choose **Go to Claude** or **Go to Foryoueats**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Accordion title="Add it yourself instead">
  In Claude, go to **Customize → Connectors → Add → Custom connector**. Name it Foryoueats, paste `https://foryoueats.com/mcp` as the address, then tap **Add** and follow step 3 above.
</Accordion>

## What to ask Claude

Try asking in your own words. For example:

| You want to | Ask Claude |
| --- | --- |
| Find a recipe | "What chicken recipes do I have?" |
| Write missing steps | "Write the steps for my banana bread." |
| Write missing ingredients | "Work out the ingredients for my grandma's soup." |
| Change a recipe | "Make my lasagna dairy-free and save it as a new recipe." |
| Save a recipe | "Save this recipe to Foryoueats." |
| Shop for a recipe | "Make a shopping list from my meatballs recipe." |
| Add groceries | "Add eggs, milk and 2 lb ground beef to my shopping list." |
| Stock your pantry | "Add what's on this receipt to my pantry." |
| Plan your week | "Plan my lasagna recipe for dinner on Tuesday." |

When something needs your choice, like which shopping list to use or which of several matching recipes you meant, Claude shows buttons to tap. You can also type your answer. Each reply has a link to open what was added in Foryoueats.

## Review Claude's changes

When you ask Claude to change one of your recipes, like writing its missing ingredients or steps, or doubling it, nothing changes straight away. Claude shows the new ingredients or steps in the chat:

- Tap **Save** to put them in your recipe.
- Want something different? Tell Claude, like "use less sugar", and it shows a new version.
- To change them yourself, tap **Edit on Foryoueats**. It opens **Edit Recipe** with them filled in. Change anything you like, then tap **Save Changes**, or **Cancel** to keep the recipe as it was.

Unsaved changes are kept for a week.

To keep the original as it is, ask Claude to save the changed version as a new recipe instead.

<Tip>
  Saved recipes follow your recipe privacy setting, like any recipe you create. Add a photo in Foryoueats to finish them off.
</Tip>

## What Claude can see

Claude can read the recipes in your library (your own, and those your friends share with you): their names, tags, times, ingredients, steps and notes. It also sees:

- What it just added, with a link to it.
- The names of your shopping lists, when it asks you to choose one.

It doesn't see your email address, pantry, meal plan or anything else in your account.

Foryoueats never sees your chats with Claude. See the [Privacy Policy](https://foryoueats.com/privacy-policy#section-8) for details.

## Disconnect Claude

Go to **Account Settings → Connected Apps** and tap **Disconnect** next to Claude. Claude loses access straight away. To remove it from Claude too, open **Customize → Connectors** in Claude, choose Foryoueats and remove it.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Claude doesn't use Foryoueats when I ask">
    Mention Foryoueats in your request, for example "Add eggs to my Foryoueats shopping list." Check the connector is turned on for the chat in Claude's tools menu.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Claude seems out of date">
    If Claude says it can't see your recipes, or uses an old way of doing something, go to **Customize → Connectors** in Claude, open Foryoueats' **⋯** menu and tap **Refresh tools list**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Things are added to the wrong account">
    Claude adds to the account you signed in with when you tapped **Connect**. Disconnect it, sign in to the right account, then connect again.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
