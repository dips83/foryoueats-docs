title: "Connect Gemini"
description: "Connect Gemini, Google's AI assistant, to Foryoueats to find and save recipes and fill your shopping lists, pantry and meal plan from a chat."
---

Connect Gemini, the AI assistant made by Google, to your Foryoueats account. Then ask Gemini in a chat and it works with your Foryoueats recipes and adds things straight to Foryoueats:

- **Find your recipes:** ask what you have, get ideas from your own recipes, or have one scaled or changed.
- **Fix up a recipe:** have Gemini write the ingredients or steps a recipe is missing, tidy them up, or scale it. Gemini gives you a link to review the changes in Foryoueats and tap **Save Changes**.
- **Save a recipe** you made or worked out together to your library.
- **Add to a shopping list:** items, or all the ingredients of a recipe in your library.
- **Stock your pantry** from a receipt, a photo or your shopping list.
- **Plan meals** on your meal planner.

Gemini can't delete anything in your account, and it only changes a recipe when you review the change and save it.

## Connect Gemini

Add Foryoueats in Gemini on the web, on your computer or in your phone's browser.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy the Foryoueats address">
    In Foryoueats, go to **Account Settings → Connected Apps**, tap **Connect Gemini** and tap **Copy**. The address is `https://foryoueats.com/mcp`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Foryoueats in Gemini">
    Tap **Open Gemini**, or go to [Gemini's connectors page](https://gemini.google.com/customize/connectors). It isn't in Gemini's menus yet, so use this link. Add a custom app and paste the address. Leave **Client ID** and **Client secret** empty, then continue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Allow it in Foryoueats">
    Sign in to Foryoueats if asked, then tap **Connect**. When it says **Gemini is connected**, choose **Go to Gemini** or **Go to Foryoueats**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What to ask Gemini

Try asking in your own words. For example:

| You want to | Ask Gemini |
| --- | --- |
| Find a recipe | "What chicken recipes do I have?" |
| Write missing steps | "Write the steps for my banana bread." |
| Write missing ingredients | "Work out the ingredients for my grandma's soup." |
| Change a recipe | "Make my lasagna dairy-free and save it as a new recipe." |
| Save a recipe | "Save this recipe to Foryoueats." |
| Shop for a recipe | "Make a shopping list from my meatballs recipe." |
| Add groceries | "Add eggs, milk and 2 lb ground beef to my shopping list." |
| Stock your pantry | "Add what's on this receipt to my pantry." |
| Plan your week | "Plan my lasagna recipe for dinner on Tuesday." |

When something needs your choice, like which shopping list to use or which of several matching recipes you meant, Gemini asks you in the chat. Each reply has a link to open what was added in Foryoueats.

## Review Gemini's changes

When you ask Gemini to change one of your recipes, like writing its missing ingredients or steps, or doubling it, nothing changes straight away. Gemini gives you a link instead:

- Open the link. It opens **Edit Recipe** with the new ingredients or steps filled in.
- Change anything you like, then tap **Save Changes**, or **Cancel** to keep the recipe as it was.
- Want something different? Tell Gemini, like "use less sugar", and it gives you a new link.

The link works for a week.

To keep the original as it is, ask Gemini to save the changed version as a new recipe instead.

<Tip>
  Saved recipes follow your recipe privacy setting, like any recipe you create. Add a photo in Foryoueats to finish them off.
</Tip>

## What Gemini can see

Gemini can read the recipes in your library (your own, and those your friends share with you): their names, tags, times, ingredients, steps and notes. It also sees:

- What it just added, with a link to it.
- The names of your shopping lists, when it asks you to choose one.

It doesn't see your email address, pantry, meal plan or anything else in your account.

Foryoueats never sees your chats with Gemini. See the [Privacy Policy](https://foryoueats.com/privacy-policy#section-8) for details.

## Disconnect Gemini

Go to **Account Settings → Connected Apps** and tap **Disconnect** next to Gemini. Gemini loses access straight away. To remove it from Gemini too, go to [Gemini's connectors page](https://gemini.google.com/customize/connectors) and remove Foryoueats.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Gemini doesn't use Foryoueats when I ask">
    Mention Foryoueats in your request, for example "Add eggs to my Foryoueats shopping list."
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Gemini asks for a client ID and secret">
    Leave them empty. If Gemini says the redirect URL was rejected, close the dialog and add the address again. If it keeps happening, contact us.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Things are added to the wrong account">
    Gemini adds to the account you signed in with when you tapped **Connect**. Disconnect it, sign in to the right account, then connect again.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
