title: "Create your free Foryoueats account"
description: "Sign up for Foryoueats in minutes using your email, Google, Facebook, or Apple account."
---

Foryoueats is completely free to use. This page walks you through creating your account and getting oriented once you're in.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the sign-up page">
    Open your browser and navigate to [foryoueats.com/register](https://foryoueats.com/register).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how you want to sign up">
    Pick any of the following sign-up methods:

    <CardGroup cols={2}>
      <Card title="Email and password" icon="mail">
        Enter your **Email**, choose a **Username** (lowercase letters, numbers, - and _; it becomes foryoueats.com/yourname), then a **Password** and **Password Confirmation**, and tap **Register**. Open the verification link we email you on the same device to unlock everything.
      </Card>
      <Card title="Login using Google" icon="/images/icons/google.svg">
        Sign up with your existing Google account — no new password needed. Then choose a username on the **Complete Registration** page and tap **Continue**.
      </Card>
      <Card title="Login using Facebook" icon="/images/icons/facebook.svg">
        Sign up with your Facebook account. Then choose a username on the **Complete Registration** page and tap **Continue**.
      </Card>
      <Card title="Login using Apple" icon="/images/icons/apple.svg">
        Sign up with your Apple ID. Works with Hide My Email if you prefer to keep your address private.
      </Card>
    </CardGroup>

    <Note>
      **Using the iPhone app?** It only has Apple and email sign-in. If you signed up with Google or Facebook on another device:

      1. Tap **Forgot your password?** and enter the email address of your Google or Facebook account.
      2. Open the email we send you and choose a new password.
      3. Back in the app, sign in with that email address and your new password.

      Your recipes will all be there.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose who sees your recipes">
    When you first sign in, we ask **Who should see the recipes you create?** Pick **Public** (everyone can see them) or **Private** (only you and the Foodie Friends you add), then tap **Continue**. You can change this anytime in **Settings → Share**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start adding recipes">
    Your account is ready. You can import a recipe from any website by pasting its URL, create a recipe manually, or explore community recipes right away. The **Getting Started** checklist walks you through your first recipe, shopping list, cookbook and meal plan.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Foryoueats supports light and dark mode. Choose **Light**, **Dark** or **System** at any time from **Menu → Settings → Account → Appearance**.
</Note>
